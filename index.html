Our Journey

When I first met you through a mutual friend, I didn't really think much of it. Meeting a friend of a friend wasn't anything unusual for me. We only exchanged a few small talks, things like "How are you?" and "What are your interests?" After that, neither of us really thought much about it. August and September passed, and we barely communicated. Then October came. You wanted to be mutuals on TikTok, and I accepted. We reconnected and suddenly had even more things to talk about. We started sending each other funny and silly videos, reacting to them with the same energy. Little by little, we grew closer, going from distant friends to people who could consider each other close.
By the middle of October, while we were both caught up in our academic lives, we still made an effort to check up on each other, making sure we were both doing alright. Perhaps that was our subtle way of showing that we wanted to know each other on a deeper level. Even during our busiest weeks, we never failed to bring each other a little encouragement. I still remember how kind and cheerful you always were whenever we talked. It made me genuinely happy to have someone who cared enough to check up on me. Someone who paid attention to even the smallest things I did. You became a kind and wonderful light in my life, bringing me a warmth I didn't know I needed.
To unwind, we decided to spend some of our free time playing games together. You invited me to a call so we could communicate better ingame, and I gladly accepted. Hearing your lovely and sweet voice for the first time made me giggle so much. I had such a wonderful time talking with you, even though I wasn't someone who talked much. We slowly grew closer, asking more about each other and sharing our likes and dislikes. I got to learn more about you, like how much you love sweets and how you can't handle spicy foods. I even wrote all of those little details down on my old phone, keeping track of the precious little things that make you, you.
Amidst our fun bonding moments and playful conversations, we gradually began opening up to each other on a deeper level. We started discussing our romantic ideals, how we wished to be loved, and the kind of care and affection we longed to receive. Perhaps it was our subtle way of expressing that we wanted something more than friendship. We exchanged little hints, both of us seemingly aware of what they meant, yet neither of us was entirely certain of the other's intentions. I remember asking you about the kind of man you would want, your preferences, and everything in between. Although I felt a little awkward bringing it up, I did my best to ask because I genuinely wanted to know whether I could be someone who matched what you were looking for. I knew this was my opportunity to learn more about you.
